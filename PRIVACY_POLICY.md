# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 7, 2026

Distil Labs Smart Home ("the App") is operated by Distil Labs.

## Data Collection

The App does not collect personal information. All text commands are processed on your device and never transmitted.

The App uses the Cactus Compute runtime for on-device model inference. Cactus Compute collects anonymous telemetry, including a randomly generated device identifier, basic device information, and inference performance metrics. This telemetry is transmitted to Cactus Compute's servers for analytics purposes. It does not include personal information, user commands, or smart home usage data.

## Data Storage

Cactus Compute stores a randomly generated device identifier and telemetry cache files in the app's cache directory on your device. All locally stored data is removed when you uninstall the App.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not include any advertising, analytics, or crash reporting services beyond the Cactus Compute runtime described above.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
